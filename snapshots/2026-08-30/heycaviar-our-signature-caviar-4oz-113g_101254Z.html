Shipping and delivery

Our signature caviar is shipped via FedEx Overnight, meaning it will arrive in 24-48 hours, at most. Our caviar is a fresh product without preservatives. We package it carefully in a styrofoam cooler, with ice packs, and upon its arrival, should be put directly into the fridge until you’re ready to enjoy!

Returns & Refunds

Something fishy? Hit us up and we’ll issue a UPS return label so you can send the jar back. You can open it, but if more than a few bites have been eaten, it can’t be refunded.
