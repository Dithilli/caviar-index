Often referred to as “River Beluga” or "Amur Sturgeon", Kaluga Caviar is the sustainable answer to the endangered Beluga or European sturgeon population. For some, consumption of Kaluga Hybrid Caviar may even exceed the exquisite culinary experience of the noble Amur Beluga Caviar variety.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Kaluga Hybrid" mean, and why is it also called "River Beluga"?

It's a cross between the Huso Dauricus (Kaluga sturgeon) and Acipenser Schrenckii (Amur sturgeon), both freshwater species from the Amur River basin. It's nicknamed "River Beluga" because the Kaluga is the world's largest freshwater sturgeon and produces a similarly large-grain, prized roe, without using true Beluga sturgeon, a species that's protected due to overfishing.

What does Kaluga Hybrid caviar taste like, and what color is it?

Expect a balanced, mild flavor with a creamy, buttery texture and a nutty finish, large-grain roe that's gentler than a briny Ossetra. Color can read as dark amber to brown with gold flecks, sometimes with a jade-like shimmer depending on the light and the individual batch, natural variation you'll see across sturgeon caviar generally.

What nutrients does Kaluga Hybrid caviar provide?

It's a source of iron, vitamin D, vitamin B12, and fatty acids, fitting the same nutrient-dense, protein-forward profile as OM's other caviars. It works well as part of an everyday-elevated meal rather than an occasional-only treat.

How should I serve Kaluga Hybrid for guests or as a gift?

Serve it over blinis or toasted brioche with a little sour cream or crème fraîche and fresh chives. For gifting, OM's Blini Gift Set pairs it with a tin opener and mother-of-pearl spoon, a ready-made presentation if you're buying it as a wellness-forward gift.

How long does Kaluga Hybrid caviar stay fresh, and how should I store it?

Store it in the coldest part of your fridge, ideally 28-32°F. Unopened, it keeps for up to three weeks; once opened, use it within 24 to 48 hours. It ships overnight in Stay-Fresh packing since it's a perishable product, so plan delivery close to when you'll serve it.
