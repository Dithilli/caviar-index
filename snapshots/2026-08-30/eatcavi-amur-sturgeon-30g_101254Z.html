    BUILD A BUNDLE & SAVE

    One of our newest CAVI Caviars - bold, balanced, and effortlessly cool. Our Amur Sturgeon delivers medium pearls with deep, glossy tones that lean from golden-amber to olive and grayish-black. Expect a rich, nutty flavor with a touch of brine and a clean, lingering finish. It’s confident without trying too hard. One bite and you’ll know exactly why it belongs in the lineup.

    Sits right between our Osetra and Kaluga Hybrid - the perfect middle ground if you want something a little richer, a little bolder, with a complex flavor that keeps you coming back for more. Easy to love, hard to put down.

    Details:

    • Origin: Farmed (Acipenser Schrenckii)

    • Texture & Flavor: Medium-sized pearls with a firm pop, offering a rich, nutty profile with subtle brine and a smooth, clean finish.

    • Packaging: Sealed for freshness in a glass jar or metal tin.

    • Storage: Keep refrigerated (below 38°F). Best enjoyed within 3–4 weeks unopened, or 3 days after opening.

    • Shipping: Perishable. Overnight delivery only.

    • Allergies: Contains fish.
    Shipping Information

    This stuff’s fancy and fresh. Your CAVI is packed to order.

    Place your order by 9:30 am EST, Monday through Thursday, and we’ll ship it overnight the same day. Orders placed after 10:00 am EST on Thursday through Sunday will ship the following Monday for Tuesday delivery.

    If you're ordering for a special occasion, please plan ahead and allow extra time for delivery. Shipping dates are not guaranteed. While you may select your desired delivery date, shipping delays can occur.

    CAVI is perishable, so we ship it overnight to keep things crisp. Expect it on your doorstep in about 1 business day. Because caviar waits for no one.
    Handling & Care

    Keep it chill. Literally.

    Unopened CAVI stays fresh for up to 3-4 weeks in the fridge (below 38°F / 3.3°C). The sooner, the fresher. Once you pop the lid? You’ve got 3 days to live your best life. Not that it’ll last that long anyway.
    Ingredients

    Fish roe, salt.
