Keluga is a special caviar, with a lot to admire: plump pearls, glittery gold in hue and buttery in texture; richly flavored, but feather-light on your tongue, with subtly sweet notes of hazelnut; a sumptuous finish that lingers on your mind. Produced from carnivorous sturgeon native to the Amur River, this caviar does everything: style and substance, boldness and charm.

Huso / Shrenckii

Huso / Shrenckii.
Hybrid of the Kaluga Sturgeon, which is a close cousin of the endangered Beluga and native to the Amur River (Southern border of Eastern Russia) system, and the Shrencki, native to the northern island of Japan. Can grow to over 1000 lb.

Key findings and nutritional facts about Pearl Street Caviar include:

1. Caviar’s exceptionally high omega-3 fatty acids content exceeds that of Sockeye and Coho salmon, but without high levels of mercury and heavy metal contaminants that may reduce Omega-3s cognitive benefit.

2. Pearl Street Caviar, in particular, is especially high in vitamin D with nearly 6x that of the USDA database average for caviar.
