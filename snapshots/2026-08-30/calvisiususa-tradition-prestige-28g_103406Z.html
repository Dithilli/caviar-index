Shipping & Delivery Guidelines

  • If your cart includes any Fresh product, only Overnight Shipping is available (to guarantee freshness).

    Fresh products: caviar, caviar gift sets, lingotto, caviar salt, crème fraîche, butter, etc.
    Non-Fresh products: blini, tinned fish, tin opener, mother-of-pearl spoon, accessories, edible gold, etc.

Processing & Transit

  • Orders placed before 11 AM EST ship same day; after 11 AM ship next business day.
  • No Saturday delivery.
  • Orders placed Friday–Sunday ship Monday.
  • No deliveries on National holidays or Mondays.
  • We do not ship to PO boxes.

Carrier & Delivery

  • Shipped via UPS Next Day Air (no signature required).
  • Drivers may withhold delivery if no one is available; redelivery must be arranged with UPS.
  • For special occasions, order at least 7 days in advance.
  • Tracking is provided upon shipment (may take a few hours to activate).
  • Once shipped, delivery timing is subject to UPS, weather, and volume conditions.

Packaging & Storage

  • Caviar ships in recyclable, compostable packaging with ice packs (temperature maintained 48–72 hours).
  • Inspect immediately upon arrival and refrigerate promptly.
  • Unopened vacuum-sealed tins keep up to 90 days at 28–40°F (store in the coldest part of the fridge).
  • Species: Pacific White sturgeon, Acipenser transmontanus
  • Origins of the species: North American West coast, from Alaska to Southern California
  • Size of the fish: large, may grow to 800 kg or 1760 lbs and can be as long as 6 m or 19’
  • Roe diameter: large, 2.8 mm/3.2 mm
  • Color: dark grey to black
  • Texture: compact and smooth, enhanced by its large roe
  • Character: balanced and elegant
  • Aromatic notes: strongly fruity with nutty and buttery notes, reminiscent of the most delicate nuances of the sea.

Flavor Notes

Tradition Prestige caviar is renowned for its large, silky eggs and elegant flavor notes. The creamy, buttery flavors are the first to arrive on the palate, followed by hints of salinity that add a subtle, oceanic dimension. The caviar finishes with an impressive, clean taste that lingers in the mouth, highlighting its luxurious quality.

Our flagship caviar is the pure expression of our caviar making legacy and craftsmanship, resulting in a delicate texture and ultimate freshness.

Recommended Pairings

Brut Champagne or Satèn (premium Franciacorta) bubbles. Serve on mother of pearl spoons as-is, to enjoy it in its purity. Or, harness Tradition’s buttery creaminess atop avocado or boiled potatoes to let its luxurious notes come forward.

Time required to harvest: 14 to 15 years

Shelf life: 90 days, unopened, from ship date
