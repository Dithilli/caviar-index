How to eat one caviar

Caviar comes with a lot of ceremony it doesn't need. Here's everything you actually have to know.

01

Take it out 20 minutes before

Let it sit out of the fridge for 20 minutes, then open it when you're ready to eat. If you want the photo, now's the time.

02

Grab a spoon

The mother-of-pearl one comes in the Kit. No spoon? The back of your hand works, too.

03

Put it on anything

A plain potato chip is the bite most people come back to. After that it goes on eggs, potatoes, pasta and fries.
