Osetra caviar is lauded worldwide for its gold sheen, silky texture, and clean finish. Our eggs deliver that taste of luxury effortlessly—marrying sustainable practices with Russian tradition. They're rich and nutty; subtly briny, with a soft shell that melts in your mouth. This is an experience that's meant to be savored. So, enjoy them straight from a mother-of-pearl spoon...and if you'd like, with a piercingly cold glass of vodka alongside.
