THE CAVIAR

Reserve Kaluga Caviar (Huso dauricus x Acipenser schrenckii) is a hybrid of Kaluga and Amur sturgeon, sustainably raised in the fresh waters of the Amur River basin. This Reserve grade features large, firm pearls with a radiant amber-to-gold shimmer. It is celebrated for its rich, buttery mouthfeel and complex flavor, with notes of hazelnut and a clean, creamy finish. Kaluga is our bestselling caviar and the one most people name as their favorite after a side-by-side tasting. From $105 for a 30g tin.

Tasting notes: Rich, buttery, and creamy with large firm pearls, hazelnut depth, and a long, clean finish.

Sourcing: Responsibly farm-raised in the Amur River basin. Like all caviar legally sold today, ours comes from regulated, CITES-compliant aquaculture rather than wild harvest.

How much do I need? Serving guide:

  • 30g: serves 1–2 (tasting for one or two)
  • 50g: serves 2–3 (date night or small plate)
  • 125g: serves 4–6 (dinner party)
  • 250g: serves 10–12 (celebration)
  • 500g: serves 20–25 (large event)

A good rule of thumb is about 15–30g per person as an appetizer.

Serving suggestions: Serve chilled over a bed of crushed ice. Use a mother-of-pearl or bone spoon to avoid metallic interference with the flavor.

Freshness & storage: Packed to order and handled with the utmost care. Keep sealed and refrigerated for 4–6 weeks. Once opened, enjoy within 48–72 hours for peak flavor.

Frequently asked questions

What does Kaluga caviar taste like?
Rich and buttery with notably large, firm pearls, a hazelnut depth and a long clean finish. Indulgent without being overpowering.

What is Kaluga hybrid caviar?
A cross of the Kaluga sturgeon and Amur sturgeon, farmed for large, firm pearls and a buttery richness. Kaluga is sometimes called "River Beluga" in the trade because of its pearl size, though it is a distinct species.

How is Reserve Kaluga different from Grand Cru Kaluga?
Grand Cru is our premium Kaluga, a higher grade with larger, glossier pearls and a silkier, longer finish. Reserve is our everyday Kaluga: rich, buttery and firm-pearled, and our bestselling tin.

How does Kaluga compare to Ossetra?
Kaluga is bolder and creamier with larger pearls; Ossetra (also spelled Osetra) is nuttier and more refined. Read the full Ossetra vs Kaluga comparison.

Where does it come from?
Responsibly managed sturgeon aquaculture in the Amur River basin, under CITES regulations.

How should I store it?
Keep sealed and refrigerated; unopened it lasts 4–6 weeks. Once opened, enjoy within 48–72 hours.

How is it shipped?
Order by 1 PM Eastern (noon Central), Monday through Thursday, and your tin ships the same day for next-day delivery. We ship Monday through Thursday only, and never on U.S. holidays, so orders around a holiday go out on the next available shipping day. Free overnight shipping on orders $89 and over.

Why a mother-of-pearl spoon?
Metal can oxidize the roe and add a metallic taste. Mother-of-pearl, bone, or glass keeps the flavor pure.

THE RITUAL

How to properly open and serve your reserve caviar.

1. CHILL & REST

Let the tin rest on ice for 10 minutes before opening to achieve the perfect texture.

2. BREAK THE SEAL

Insert a caviar key into the groove of the tin and gently twist to pop the vacuum seal.

3. THE PERFECT BITE

Use a mother of pearl spoon to scoop the pearls onto a blini or your favorite potato chip.

Caviére 101

How is it shipped? +
We ship every order overnight in a custom insulated cooler packed with gel ice. Order by 1 PM Eastern (noon Central), Monday through Thursday, and it ships the same day. Shipping is free on orders of $89 or more, otherwise a flat $35.
How long will my caviar stay fresh? +
Unopened, your tin keeps for 4 to 6 weeks in the coldest part of your refrigerator, between 28°F and 38°F. Once opened, enjoy it within 48 to 72 hours.
What is the best way to serve it? +
Keep it simple. Serve it chilled over ice. Use a mother of pearl, bone, or wooden spoon, never metal, as it can oxidize the pearls and alter the pure taste.
