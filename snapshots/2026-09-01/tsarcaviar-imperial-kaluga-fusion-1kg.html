The largest pearls. The richest finish. Imperial Kaluga Fusion is a hybrid masterpiece — born from Kaluga and Amur sturgeon, its oversized brown pearls deliver a smooth, creamy, buttery flavor that lingers long after the last bite. The ultimate expression of Amur River luxury.

Imperial Kaluga Fusion Caviar is produced through a rare hybridization of Kaluga (Huso Dauricus) and Amur Sturgeon (Acipenser Shrenckii) — two of the most prestigious sturgeon species in the world. The result is a caviar of extraordinary size and refinement: large brown pearls ranging from 0.12 to 0.13 inches, each one delivering a smooth, creamy texture and a rich buttery flavor with a long, elegant finish. Farmed in the pristine Amur River region and harvested at peak maturity, Imperial Kaluga Fusion is a globally sought-after delicacy that satisfies even the most discerning connoisseurs. Rich in Omega-3 fatty acids and essential vitamins. Delivered fresh overnight across the USA in premium packaging. Free shipping on orders over $300.

Tasting Notes

Flavor: Rich, buttery, with a clean briny finish. Hints of nuttiness and the sea.
Texture: Firm, glossy pearls with a satisfying pop.
Color: Deep amber to obsidian black, varying by sturgeon species and grade.

Origin & Source

Sourced directly from heritage sturgeon farms across the Caspian Basin and Aquitaine region. CITES-certified, raised under sustainable aquaculture practices that preserve the centuries-old caviar tradition.

Serving Recommendations

  • Pairings: Champagne (Brut), Beluga vodka, chilled blini, crème fraîche, chopped egg, finely diced red onion
  • Servingware: Always use a mother-of-pearl, horn, or bone spoon — never metal (oxidation alters flavor)
  • Temperature: Serve chilled at 28-32°F (-2 to 0°C)
  • Portion: Plan for 15-30g per guest as appetizer; 50-100g for a tasting experience

Storage

Refrigerate immediately upon arrival at 28-32°F (coldest part of fridge). Best consumed within 3 weeks of opening; sealed tins keep up to 8 weeks. Never freeze.
