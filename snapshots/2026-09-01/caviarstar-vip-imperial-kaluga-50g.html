Description

This 50g tin of VIP Kaluga Hybrid Caviar is the same top-shelf Kaluga served by discerning chefs and Michelin restaurants across the country... like the incredible Chubby Fish in Charleston, SC! Make your own rendition of their famous Caviar Sammich at home or simply enjoy on buttered bread, blinis and creme fraiche, potato chips, fried chicken, buttery pasta, seasoned rice, or even ice cream. The world is your oyster with this premium, versatile, beautiful caviar.

More about the Caviar: 

The farm-raised "River Beluga" Kaluga Caviar is a sustainable hybrid of Amur River sturgeons Kaluga (Huso dauricus) and Schrenckii (Acipenser schrenckii). The highest grade of Kaluga in the market, this caviar features exceptionally extra-large grains, truly remarkable in size for sturgeon, and a lighter golden-brown color. Beyond its visual appeal, this Kaluga Hybrid caviar offers a delicate, buttery flavor with low salinity and a subtle hint of earth on the finish. These exquisite large beads, with their smooth, rich taste make this caviar a consistent favorite around the world.

Each jar is hand-packed and carefully inspected for optimal color, size, freshness, flavor, and texture. For additional details on shipping, usage, and privacy policies, please visit our Policy Page Policy Page.
