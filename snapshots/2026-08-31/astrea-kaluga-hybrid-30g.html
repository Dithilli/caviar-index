PRODUCT DETAIL

Our Kaluga Hybrid caviar is a striking expression of richness and refinement, distinguished by its large, luminous pearls and tones ranging from pearl gray to warm golden brown. Each bead delivers a pronounced pop, unfolding into layers of creamy butter, delicate brine, and lingering umami.

Sourced from a prized cross between Huso dauricus and Acipenser schrenckii sturgeon, this exceptional caviar is harvested between 12 to 15 years maturity and aged for two to four months before packing to develop its signature depth and silkiness.

The result is a bold yet balanced caviar with a lush texture and full-bodied finish — equally compelling enjoyed on its own or paired with refined accompaniments.

Each tin is hand-packed at our facility in Los Angeles, California.

Serve traditionally with blinis and crème fraîche, or elevate dishes such as seafood pasta, oysters, and deviled eggs with its rich, buttery complexity.

Available in 30g, 50g, 100g, 200g, and 550g tins.
Mother-of-pearl spoon and tin opener sold separately.

Complimentary shipping on orders over $300.

What makes Astrea Caviar so special?

Astrea caviar

  • Astrea Caviar is used by Award-Winning chefs all over the world.
  • There are no added preservative and no added coloring to the caviar. Our caviar has two ingredients, sturgeon roe and salt. We work with our sustainable farmers to provide the highest quality all natural products to your door.
  • Proprietary extended aging process of our Astrea Caviar roe for exceptional and complex taste
  • We only harvest mature sturgeon that are 12-18 years of age (species dependent) which allows for the largest and best quality roe possible.
  • Direct tracing of each sturgeon from fish to tin to ensure full quality control.
  • Highest quality French manufactured pressure sealed tins for optimal freshness.

Saturday Delivery
Saturday delivery is available to most locations within the continental United States for an additional $25 fee. At this time, Saturday delivery is not available for Alaska and Hawaii.

Shipping Methods & Time:

We ship Monday through Thursday, Friday shipping for Saturday delivery for the above mentioned fee. We ship all days outside of Federal Holidays.

A shipping confirmation with tracking information will be sent via email once your order has been processed.
