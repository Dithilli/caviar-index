Royal Ossetra Caviar from One Caviar is what happens when elegance loosens its tie. Think medium to large pearls with a luminous golden glow and a flavor that knows how to linger without overstaying its welcome. Buttery, gently briny, quietly savory. 

Sourced from premium farmed sturgeon and prepared using traditional malossol techniques, this caviar is lightly cured so the good stuff gets to shine. Each tiny bead melts in the mouth with notes of sea breeze and deep umami, the kind of flavor that makes people pause mid bite and say, “Oh… wow.” It’s no surprise Ossetra is one of the most loved and talked about caviars in the world.

This is caviar that plays well with others. Spoon it onto blinis with crème fraîche, eat it straight from the tin with a mother of pearl spoon, or let it crash your favorite comfort foods. Potatoes. Eggs. Pasta. Even a late night snack moment. It’s equally at home at a dinner party or a very personal celebration for one.

A Caviar That Is Also a Work of Art
