Suggested Portions

Here’s a quick guide for how much caviar to serve:

Grams - Serves About:

  • 30 g - 1–2 people
  • 50 g - 2–4 people
  • 125 g - 4–8 people
  • 250 g - 10–20 people
  • 500 g - 20–40 people
  • 1 kg - 40-80 people
Storage and Shelf Life

Our caviar is packed fresh, so it should be kept cold and handled carefully.

  • Temperature: Ideally 28–32°F (–2 to 0 °C).
  • Freezing: We don’t recommend freezing caviar, as it can damage the delicate texture.
  • Unopened tins: Store in the coldest part of your fridge and use within 1–3 weeks (check the “best by” date on each tin).
  • Opened tins: For the best taste and texture, enjoy within 2 days.

Serving Caviar

Keep caviar chilled in the refrigerator until you’re ready to serve. Once opened, it’s best enjoyed within 2–3 days. To present it properly, leave the caviar in its original tin and place the tin in a bowl of ice so it stays cool. Avoid using metal spoons, as they can affect the taste—opt for our mother-of-pearl caviar spoon or horn.

Classic Pairings

Caviar is delicious on its own, but it also pairs beautifully with a range of foods and drinks, such as:

  • Mini pancakes
  • Fresh cheese
  • Crème fraîche or sour cream
  • Onion or chives
  • Capers
  • Finely chopped egg
  • Champagne or dry white wine
  • Vodka (served icy cold)
