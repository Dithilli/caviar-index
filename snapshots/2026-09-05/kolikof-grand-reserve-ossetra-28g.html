    The Ritual

    The perfect tasting in three steps

    Caviar rewards a little ceremony. Here's how to get the most from every pearl.

    ❄

    Chill

    Keep the tin nestled in ice and open it just before serving. Caviar is at its best served cold, never room temperature.

    🥄

    Spoon

    Use the included Mother-of-Pearl spoon — never metal, which can bruise the pearls and add a tinny note. Lift a small, generous mound.

    ✨

    Savor

    Roll the pearls across your palate, then press gently to release a clean burst of the sea. Take your time — this is gastronomy.
